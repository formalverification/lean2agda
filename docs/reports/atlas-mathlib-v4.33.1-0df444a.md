   `eq_def`), names ending in a `match_`, `proof_` or `unsafe_`
   component, a constructor's `injEq` and `noConfusion`, an inductive
   type's `casesOn`, `recOn`, `below` and the like, and a structure's
   projections to its parents.  It does not hold of a recursor `rec`, a
   constructor, or a structure's own fields.  It judges a private name
   by the name it was written with; Lean's own

   generated.  Of Mathlib's 84,774 private constants, 80,026 are
   auto-generated by Batteries' test and 4,748 are not.

## Where `#print axioms` falls short

`Lean.collectAxioms`, which `#print axioms` calls, caches the axioms of
a constant it reaches while one of that constant's own dependencies is
still being visited, counting that dependency as having none.  The
cycles where this happens are an inductive type and its constructors (a
constructor's type mentions its type, and the type depends on its
constructors) and mutually recursive definitions such as the
`_unsafe_rec` companions of mutual `partial def`s.  Each `.olean` stores
a table of the axioms of its exported declarations computed this way,
and an importing module trusts it (Lean issue [15226]; pull request
[15385], open, would fix it for inductive types only).  The atlas
computes reachability over the same edges instead, and records in
`lean_omits` what Lean leaves out.  Lean never reports an axiom the
atlas does not; the program checks that for every constant.

+  1,837 constants of the environment, 550 of them in Mathlib, get an
   incomplete axiom set from Lean.  The axiom left out is `Quot.sound`
   for 1,361, `propext` for 1,359 and `Classical.choice` for 833 of
   them.
+  Mathlib's 550 are 203 definitions, 194 theorems, 78 constructors, 65
   inductive types, 9 recursors and 1 opaque constant.
+  Every axiom Lean leaves out here is one of the standard three, though
   the same mechanism can hide `sorryAx` (`docs/lean-findings.md`, LF-1,
   has a two-file reproduction).
+  With Lean's axiom sets, Mathlib's tiers would be T0 84,133, T1
   120,221, T2 364,794 and T3 41.  The Mathlib constants whose tier
   changes are as follows:

| T0 | T2 | 129 |
| T1 | T2 | 267 |

### Mendoza-Smith

+  **What it counts**.  In the paper's repository
   (`scripts/build_decl_graph.py`), its "Mathlib declarations" are every
   constant of the environment of `import Lean` and `import Mathlib`
   whose name is not internal: Lean's core and Mathlib's other
   dependencies are included, and constants with a component that begins
   with an underscore (`_proof_1`, `_private`) are not.  A declaration
   is classical when `Classical.choice` is reachable from it in a graph
   whose only edges run from the value of a definition or theorem to the
   non-internal constants the value uses (`src/lean/kernel_graph.py`).
+  **Which revision**.  Mathlib `9f0aee2`, master of 2026-03-28 on Lean
   v4.29.0-rc8, which the repository's `scripts/setup_mathlib.sh` pins;
   v4.33.1 is 3,558 commits later.
+  **Reproduced**.  `atlas/probes/ValueGraph.lean`, which implements the
   method, run at `9f0aee2` under Lean v4.29.0-rc8 with Mathlib's cache
   for that commit, gives 171,523 of 471,261: one more of each than
   published.  The cause of the difference of one was not found; the
   paper's graph file is not published.
+  **The difference from this atlas**.  At v4.33.1 (0df444a) the method
   gives 184,823 of 493,504 (37.5 percent); the population has grown by
   22,243 since `9f0aee2`.  The exact count on the same population is
   306,754 (62.2 percent): the method misses 121,931 constants that
   depend on `Classical.choice`.  Lifting its three restrictions, alone
   and together, shows where they are, as follows:

| restrictions lifted | classical at 9f0aee2 | classical at v4.33.1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| none (the method) | 171,523 | 184,823 |
| edges from types | 173,127 | 187,150 |
| edges out of the other kinds | 190,336 | 203,128 |

Paths through internal names matter most: a proof that reaches
`Classical.choice` only through an auxiliary lemma (`_proof_1`) or a
private declaration does not make its theorem classical.  The method's
"constructive" therefore means that no path through public definitions
and theorems reaches the axiom, and its classical count is a lower bound
on dependence on `Classical.choice`, not a count of it.
