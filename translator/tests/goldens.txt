# File: translator/tests/goldens.txt
#
# The trees translator/scripts/goldens.sh translates and checks against
# tests/expected/scaffolds/NAME/ (issue M2-9; docs/performance.md): one
# per line, the golden's name, its form, then the arguments of
# `lake exe lean2agda --out DIR`.  The form is `tree` for a golden that
# holds the emitted modules, and `digest` for one that holds each
# module's digest and size in modules.txt instead, for a tree too large
# to commit; both hold the library file, refusals.txt when the
# translator refuses anything, and the refusal loop's listings.  The
# corpus entries are named by their ids in corpus/manifest.json; the
# first three are also checked by run.sh, on every pull request.  Three
# entries are not here yet: finset-card-image, subgroup-card-dvd and
# real-sqrt-nonneg, whose loops do not finish under the checker's bounds
# (docs/performance.md, "The three largest closures"); issue M2-15 adds
# them.
